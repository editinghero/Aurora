# Aurora Music Player 🎵

A beautiful, modern music player for your local audio files with powerful audio controls and stunning visuals.

![Aurora Music Player](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-1.0.0-blue)
![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%20%7C%20Web-lightgrey)

## ✨ Features

### Audio Playback
- **Local File Support** - Play MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, and OGG files
- **Queue Management** - Build and organize your playlist with drag-and-drop reordering
- **Search** - Quickly find tracks in your queue by title, artist, or album
- **Play History** - Keep track of recently played songs
- **Shuffle & Repeat** - Standard playback controls with repeat one/all modes

### Audio Enhancement
- **10-Band Equalizer** - Fine-tune your audio with professional-grade EQ
- **Speed Control** - Adjust playback speed from 0.5x to 2x with pitch preservation
- **Reverb Effects** - Add depth and ambiance to your music

### Visual Experience
- **Dynamic Themes** - Choose from 8 beautiful color themes or enable auto-theme
- **Album Artwork** - Display album art from your music files (desktop app)
- **Visualizer** - Pulse and bar visualizers that react to your music
- **Smooth Animations** - Polished UI with gradient effects and transitions

### Desktop App Exclusive
- **Music Library** - Scan folders and build a persistent music library with SQLite
- **Mini Player** - Compact always-on-top player window for multitasking
- **Offline First** - All your music stays on your computer, no cloud required

### Web App (PWA)
- **Progressive Web App** - Install on any device from your browser
- **Works Offline** - Play your music without an internet connection
- **Cross-Platform** - Works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Desktop App

1. **Download** the portable or installer version from releases
2. **Run** the application
3. **Add Music**:
   - Click "Open Folder" button
   - Choose "Clear Queue & Play" or "Add to Queue"
   - Select your music folder
   - Wait for scanning to complete
4. **Play** - Your music will start automatically!

### Managing Your Library

- **Open Folder** - Scan a new folder and choose how to add tracks
- **Library** - Load previously scanned tracks from database
- **Plus Button** - Add individual songs with "Play Now" or "Add to Queue"
- **Queue** - View, search, and reorder your current playlist
- **History** - See your recently played tracks

### Mini Player

1. Click the minimize icon in the bottom navbar
2. A compact player window appears
3. Control playback without the main window
4. Click the red X to close mini player

### Audio Controls

- **Equalizer** - Adjust 10 frequency bands or use presets
- **Effects** - Control playback speed and reverb intensity
- **Themes** - Pick a color theme or enable auto-theme for variety

## 🎨 Themes

Choose from 8 beautiful themes:
- Ember (Orange/Red)
- Ocean (Blue)
- Forest (Green)
- Sunset (Pink/Purple)
- Lavender (Purple)
- Rose (Pink)

Enable **Auto Theme** to randomly change themes with each song!

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Space** - Play/Pause
- **Arrow Left** - Previous track
- **Arrow Right** - Next track
- **Swipe Left/Right** - Skip tracks (touch devices)
- **Swipe Up** - Open queue (touch devices)

## 🔧 System Requirements

### Desktop App
- **OS**: Windows 10 or later (64-bit)
- **RAM**: 4GB minimum
- **Storage**: 100MB for app + space for music library database

### Web App
- **Browser**: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari (latest versions)
- **Storage**: Depends on browser cache settings

## 💡 Tips

- **Desktop**: Use "Open Folder" to scan your entire music collection once, then use "Library" to load instantly
- **Search**: Use the search bar in the queue to quickly find songs
- **Mini Player**: Perfect for keeping music controls visible while working
- **Auto Theme**: Enable for a fresh look with every song
- **Equalizer Presets**: Start with a preset and fine-tune to your taste

## 🐛 Known Issues

- Large music libraries (10,000+ tracks) may take time to scan initially
- Album artwork extraction requires proper ID3 tags in your music files
- Web app cannot access local file system directly (browser limitation)
